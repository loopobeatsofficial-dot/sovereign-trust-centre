Your household record deserves restraint.

This policy explains what Sovereign collects, why it is used, where processing occurs, and the choices available to you. Effective October 1, 2026.

Plain-language promise.

Sovereign does not ask for online-banking passwords or full account numbers. It does not sell personal information or use financial records for advertising.

1. Who is responsible

Sovereign is operated by IronWolfProductions in Canada. The accountable privacy contact is Ironwolfproductions@gmail.com. This policy is designed around Canadian private-sector privacy principles, including accountability, identified purposes, consent, limiting collection, safeguards, access and correction.

2. Information you choose to provide

Identity and accessEmail address, an optional username, authentication identifiers, optional two-step (authenticator app) enrolment, session information and accepted household invitations.
Household profilesDisplay name, relationship, province or territory, date of birth, dependent status and optional notes.
Financial planning recordsAccount type, institution label, optional final 2–4 account digits, balances derived from entered records, contributions, withdrawals, income and expenses.
Collection activityAchievement progress, card ownership, upgrades, trades and provenance.
Strategist requestYour current question, chosen Canadian jurisdiction and a minimized aggregate household snapshot only after consent.
Technical operationsSecurity, synchronization, quota and error information needed to provide and protect the service. Private questions and financial payloads are not written to application logs.

3. What we deliberately do not collect

  • Online-banking usernames, passwords, PINs or security answers.
  • Full bank or credit-card account numbers.
  • Social Insurance Numbers through ordinary Sovereign features.
  • Advertising profiles or data for sale to data brokers.
  • Strategist conversation history as a stored cloud chat history.

4. Purposes and consent

Information is used to authenticate you, maintain an encrypted local-first household record, synchronize authorized household data, calculate user-requested planning summaries, operate achievement cards, answer a Strategist question you deliberately submit, prevent abuse, provide support and meet legal obligations. Sovereign asks for explicit consent before sending a minimized snapshot to Strategist. For a relevant question, the app may separately offer to include broad work context and confirmed receipt totals by tax year and category for that question only. This optional summary is omitted unless you choose to include it. A receipt image and the merchant, date, currency, category and amounts you confirm may also be copied to your private Supabase storage only when you explicitly choose the private cloud-copy action. You may withhold either permission and continue using the rest of the app.

5. Local-first storage and service providers

On supported devices, the local database is protected with SQLCipher and a key stored through the platform keychain. Authorized household records may also synchronize over encrypted network connections through PowerSync and Supabase so approved devices and household members remain consistent. You sign in with an email address and password (optionally with a username instead of the email), a six-digit code emailed to you, or Sign in with Apple, and you may add optional two-step verification using an authenticator app. Authentication is operated through Supabase; transactional authentication emails (sign-in, sign-up confirmation and password-reset codes) are delivered by Resend, our email service provider, which processes your email address, the one-time code and basic message metadata for that purpose. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple verifies your identity, and when you later delete your account Sovereign asks Apple to revoke that authorization. A Strategist request is processed server-side using OpenAI only after consent; names, birth dates, institutions, raw transactions, account identifiers and conversation history are excluded from its designed snapshot. Even when the optional work and receipt summary is chosen, receipt images, OCR text, merchant details, exact receipt dates, exact job titles and individual purchases remain excluded from Strategist.

When you explicitly enable a private cloud copy for an eligible receipt, the receipt image and the confirmed merchant, date, currency, category and amount fields are stored in an owner-bound private Supabase storage path and record. Household members cannot access that copy. OCR text and work notes remain local and are not synchronized through this feature. Removing the copy or deleting the account queues removal and completes it when a connection is available; a download link already issued may remain usable for up to five minutes. OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models. Under its standard API controls, submitted content may appear in abuse-monitoring logs retained for up to 30 days, unless a different approved data-retention control applies. These providers process information under their own infrastructure and may process data outside your province or Canada, where it can be subject to foreign law. Sovereign limits the information sent according to the purpose of each service.

6. Household sharing

Household data is shared only with authenticated members authorized for that household. Invitations are email-bound, time-limited and single-use. Accepting an invitation requires an explicit action by the invited adult. Information is not intentionally shared across households. Do not add another adult’s information unless you have authority to do so.

7. Children and dependents

A child or dependent entry is a profile maintained by an authorized adult; it is not an app account and does not imply that the child consented. Collect only what is genuinely necessary for household planning. An owner can archive an offline dependent profile or choose permanent erasure of that profile and its dependent records. Linked adult identities remain controlled by those adults.

8. Retention, archiving and erasure

Active records remain while needed to provide Sovereign. Archiving hides an offline profile from active selectors but preserves its financial history and can be reversed. Permanent erasure removes the selected unlinked profile and dependent financial and collection records and cannot be undone. Some narrowly required security, legal or backup records may persist for a limited period where permitted or required; restored backups are subject to the same protections and deletion cycle.

9. Safeguards

Sovereign uses local database encryption, platform key storage, transport encryption, household-scoped authorization, row-level security, minimized AI snapshots, bounded inputs and non-secret logging. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm, IronWolfProductions will assess notification and reporting obligations under applicable law.

10. Camera, receipts and work profile

If you choose Scan receipt, iOS provides a document camera and on-device text recognition. By default, the receipt image, recognized text, verified amounts and exact profession are stored in Sovereign’s encrypted local database on that device. The app can offer an explicit private cloud-copy action for an eligible receipt; that action sends only the image and the confirmed merchant, date, currency, category and amount fields to the owner-bound private Supabase copy described above. OCR text, work notes and exact profession remain local and are excluded from that copy. Sovereign autofills a field only when recognition confidence is at least 50 percent; lower-confidence fields are left blank. OCR can still misread numbers, so Sovereign requires you to review the image and confirm every field. Deleting a local receipt removes its local copy; removing an enabled private cloud copy queues deletion of that copy when a connection is available. Sovereign does not treat a profession as a tax code or guarantee that an expense is deductible.

11. Your choices and rights

You may request access to or correction of personal information under IronWolfProductions’ control, withdraw consent where legally available, archive or erase eligible offline profiles through the app, or delete your account in the app (open Vault, then account controls, then Delete Sovereign account). Removing a linked adult member’s access is not a self-serve action in the app, because a linked adult controls their own login identity and consent; contact us if you need help with such a request. Certain information may be retained where law requires or permits it. To make a privacy request, email Ironwolfproductions@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity without asking you to send banking credentials.

12. Changes and complaints

Material changes will be identified through an updated effective date and, where appropriate, an in-app notice or renewed consent. Contact us first so we can investigate. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the applicable provincial privacy regulator.
