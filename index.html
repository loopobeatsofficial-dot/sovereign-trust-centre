Contact

Email Ironwolfproductions@gmail.com with “Sovereign support” in the subject. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, the screen involved and a description of what you expected and observed.

Never email sensitive credentials.

Do not send passwords, email sign-in codes, invitation tokens, API keys, recovery codes, PINs, full account numbers, Social Insurance Numbers, private keys or screenshots containing private financial information. Support will never ask for them.

Before contacting support

  1. Confirm your network connection, then reopen Sovereign; the encrypted local record should remain available offline.
  2. For email sign-in codes, request a fresh code and enter it in Sovereign on this device; codes expire and are single-use.
  3. For synchronization, leave the app open briefly after reconnecting. Do not reinstall before asking for help if local changes may still be pending.
  4. For deletion problems, resolve pending household card trades and confirm you are the household owner.

Urgent security or privacy concern

Use the subject “Sovereign security” or “Sovereign privacy.” Describe the affected feature and approximate time without including private payloads. IronWolfProductions will triage the report and provide safe instructions for any additional evidence.

Financial guidance

Sovereign and Strategist provide educational information. They are not emergency, legal, accounting, tax or regulated investment services. Verify important decisions with the relevant Canadian authority and a qualified professional.
